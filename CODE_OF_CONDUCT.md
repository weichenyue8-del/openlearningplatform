# OpenAP community code of conduct

## Our commitment

OpenAP is built for learners. Contributors and maintainers commit to a
welcoming, safe, and respectful community regardless of age, ability, body
size, education, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level,
nationality, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction and responsibility.
- Give specific feedback about work, never personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Protect minors and never solicit personal information from students.
- Put learner safety, accessibility, and factual accuracy ahead of speed.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable. Trolling or repeated disruption is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or private security-reporting channel. Do not include a target's private
information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good faith,
protect confidentiality where possible, and may edit or remove content, issue
a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone officially
represents OpenAP. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is itself
a violation.
